What to include

When contacting support, please include:

  • the device you are using, such as iPhone, iPad, or Mac;
  • your iOS, iPadOS, or macOS version;
  • the ExpenseFlow app version;
  • a short description of what happened;
  • screenshots, if they help explain the issue; and
  • whether the issue happens every time or only sometimes.

Common questions

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. ExpenseFlow is designed for manual, private tracking. You can record expenses, income, budgets, debts, lending, savings goals, and group splits without connecting a bank account.

Where is my data stored?

Core records are stored on your device unless you choose a feature that uses another service, such as cloud sync, backup, receipt scanning, or the optional AI assistant. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Subscriptions and purchases are managed through your Apple Account. Open the App Store or Settings app, go to your Apple Account subscriptions, and choose ExpenseFlow to manage or cancel your plan.

How do I restore purchases?

Open ExpenseFlow, go to the Premium or subscription screen, and use Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my account or cloud data?

If you use an account or cloud features, you can delete your account and cloud data from ExpenseFlow settings where available. If you cannot complete the request in the app, email support and we will help verify and process the request.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us with the details. Bug reports are most useful when they include the device, system version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and screenshots if possible.
